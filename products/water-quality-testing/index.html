Water Quality Testing Equipment in Oman

From a single pocket tester to a complete water laboratory, IGI supplies the instruments Oman's utilities, consultants and industries use to test drinking water, wastewater, groundwater and seawater. Our range covers portable, benchtop and online meters for pH, conductivity, TDS, salinity, dissolved oxygen, ORP and turbidity, plus complete systems for BOD and COD analysis and spectrophotometers.
