Questions

Forced or natural convection oven?

Forced convection gives faster drying and better temperature uniformity. Natural convection is gentler for light powders and samples that must not be disturbed.

Are JSR ovens in stock?

Yes. Several JSR ovens, incubators and water baths are in stock in Muscat.
