IGI supplies complete systems for BOD and COD analysis for wastewater treatment plants and environmental laboratories in Oman. Questions

How does the OxiTop BOD system work?

OxiTop heads measure the pressure drop in a sealed bottle as bacteria use oxygen, giving BOD directly in mg/l without titration or dilution series.

Do you supply COD reagents and cell tests?

Tell us the test range you need and we will quote the matching cell tests and reagents together with the thermoreactor.
