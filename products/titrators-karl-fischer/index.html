Questions

Coulometric or volumetric Karl Fischer?

Coulometric KF suits very low water content (ppm level), for example in fuels and transformer oil. Volumetric KF suits higher water content in food and chemicals.

Do you supply titration electrodes?

Yes. We supply SI Analytics electrodes for acid-base, redox and other titrations.
