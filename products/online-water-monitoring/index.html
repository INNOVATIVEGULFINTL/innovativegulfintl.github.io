Online Wastewater Monitoring – WTW IQ SensorNet

WTW IQ SensorNet XT is a modular online system for continuous wastewater monitoring. One cable carries power and communications, modules stack without extra wiring, and the system expands from 1 to 20 sensors with system-wide lightning protection and a 3-year warranty. IGI supplies, installs and services IQ SensorNet systems and IDS sensors in Oman.
