Questions

What is the difference between a laminar flow and a biosafety cabinet?

A laminar flow cabinet protects the sample with clean filtered air. A biosafety cabinet protects the sample, the user and the environment, and is required for work with microorganisms.

Do you install and certify cabinets?

Yes. We deliver, install and test cabinets at your laboratory.
