Questions

Optical or galvanic DO sensor?

Optical sensors such as WTW FDO need almost no maintenance and no membrane changes. Galvanic sensors such as CellOx cost less and suit occasional measurements.

Can one meter measure DO, pH and conductivity?

Yes. WTW Multi IDS meters accept digital DO, pH and conductivity sensors on the same meter.
