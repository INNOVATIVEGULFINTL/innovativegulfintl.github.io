Food & Dairy Testing Equipment in Oman

IGI supplies rapid food testing analysers and dairy fat testing equipment to food factories, dairies and food safety laboratories in Oman. CDR FoodLab analysers measure free fatty acids, peroxide value, anisidine value, alkaline phosphatase, lactose, lactic acid and more in oils, dairy, eggs, bakery, tomato products and feed. Funke Gerber centrifuges and butyrometers cover the Gerber fat method for milk, butter and cheese.
