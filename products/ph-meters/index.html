Questions

Which pH meter should I buy for field testing in Oman?

For field work choose a waterproof portable meter such as the WTW pH 3110 or a Milwaukee pocket tester. For laboratory work a benchtop meter with a refillable electrode gives better accuracy. Tell us your samples and we will recommend a model.

Do you supply pH buffer and calibration solutions?

Yes. We keep pH 4.01, 7.01 and 10.01 buffers, electrode storage and cleaning solutions in stock in Muscat.

Can IGI calibrate and service pH meters?

Yes. We install, calibrate and service the meters we supply, and we supply replacement electrodes.
