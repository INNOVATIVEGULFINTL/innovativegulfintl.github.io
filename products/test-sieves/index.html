Test Sieves & Sieve Shakers in Oman

IGI supplies test sieves and sieve shakers for particle size analysis of aggregates, soil, cement, sand, flour and powders. The range includes Endecotts test sieves, sieve shakers and air-jet sieving from the UK, and Glenammer stainless steel test sieves with mesh or perforated plate, with several 300 mm sieves in stock in Muscat.
