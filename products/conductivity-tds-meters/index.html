Questions

Do you have TDS meters for RO and drinking water?

Yes. Milwaukee pocket EC/TDS testers and WTW Cond meters cover drinking water, RO permeate and high-salinity samples.

Which conductivity standard should I use?

1413 µS/cm is the most common check standard. We also supply 0.01 mol/l KCl standard for WTW meters.
