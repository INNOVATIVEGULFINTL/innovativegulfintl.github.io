Environmental & Hydrographic Monitoring in Oman

For ocean, coastal, surface water and groundwater studies, IGI supplies Xylem's environmental brands: YSI water quality sondes and data platforms, Aanderaa sensors and instruments, SonTek acoustic Doppler instruments for flow measurement in rivers, canals and harbours, and HYPACK hydrographic survey software. For wells and boreholes we stock Spohr water level meters from 30 m to 500 m.
