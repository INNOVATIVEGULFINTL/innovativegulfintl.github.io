Questions

Which Atago PAL should I choose?

PAL-1 reads Brix 0.0–53.0 % for most juices and drinks. PAL-3 covers the full range up to 93.0 % Brix for syrups and jams. PAL Salt measures salt content.

Are Atago refractometers in stock?

Yes. PAL-1, PAL-3 and PAL Salt are in stock in Muscat.
